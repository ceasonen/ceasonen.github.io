  We decompose prompt faithfulness into checks for subject, attribute, count, relation, style, and anatomy. The study provides human rubric labels for 2,400 prompt-image pairs, evidence and counterfactual subsets, and a cross-dataset stress test; under the same frozen CLIP backbone, rubric-specific checking improves macro-F1 from 0.8762 to 0.8836.
