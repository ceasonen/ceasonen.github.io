abstract: >-
  We study whether a neural operator can expose a useful risk signal from one forward pass. The method reads allocation entropy from the internal prediction path and selectively escalates high-risk cases; on electromagnetic scattering, it improves Spearman correlation from 0.61 to 0.67 and failure-detection AUROC from 0.78 to 0.82 at 1.13× batched cost.
