DeepYOLO

YOLO maps images to compact grammar-constrained scene reports; a text-only LLM answers VQAv2 without multimodal training — 42.3% accuracy, 2.5× fewer tokens than scene-graph serialization. Corresponding author.

ICANN 2026 2026

Sequential RAG controller that chooses support, refute, or stop to counter support drift under a fixed budget — 87.4% vs 80.8% on 440 examples, 2.4× fewer retrieval tokens. Corresponding author.
