\cvsection{Selected Research Experience}
\textbf{OV-OrthKD: Open-Vocabulary Audio-Visual Event Localization}\hfill Co-first author
\begin{itemize}
\item Separated strong visual supervision from weaker audio supervision using orthogonal feature paths, with text prototypes for open-vocabulary transfer.
\item Reached \textbf{0.816 segment AP} on OV-AVEBench. F1@0.5 improved by \textbf{2.7 points} overall and \textbf{3.4 points} on unseen categories over the official fine-tuning baseline.
\end{itemize}
\par\addvspace{4pt}
\textbf{OrthKD: Lightweight Diabetic Retinopathy Screening}\hfill Co-first author
\begin{itemize}
\item Transferred complementary knowledge from heterogeneous teachers while restricting weaker-teacher supervision to features and reducing redundancy through orthogonality constraints.
\item Evaluated on \textbf{132,049} retinal images. A \textbf{5.4M}-parameter student achieved \textbf{0.885 QWK} on EyePACS and improved zero-shot Messidor-2 QWK from \textbf{0.507 to 0.728} (quadratic weighted kappa).
\end{itemize}
\par\addvspace{4pt}
\textbf{DeepYOLO: Symbolic Visual Reasoning}\hfill Corresponding author\\
Proposed the initial research idea and collaborated with a junior student, who led implementation and experiments, on converting images into scene descriptions for text-only language models.
