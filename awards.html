IJCNN 2026 2026
DeepYOLO

We convert images into grammar-constrained scene descriptions for visual question answering with text-only LLMs. DeepYOLO achieves 42.3% accuracy on VQAv2 with 2.5× fewer prompt tokens than scene-graph serialization. Corresponding author.
