Selected Research and Publications

2026

Neural-Operator Risk Assessment NeurIPS 2026 · Under Review

This first-author submission grew from research I began in my second year. It studies how to assess the reliability of neural-operator predictions from a single forward pass.

2026

OV-OrthKD ACM MM 2026 · Oral

Our ACM MM paper studies open-vocabulary audio-visual event localization when visual and audio teachers differ in reliability. I am a co-first author, and the paper was selected for an oral presentation. Wenzhuo Lei is the corresponding author.

2026

OrthKD IJCAI 2026 · Oral

Our IJCAI paper studies knowledge distillation from heterogeneous clinical models for diabetic retinopathy screening. I am a co-first author, and the paper was selected for an oral presentation.

2026

DeepYOLO IJCNN 2026

This paper grew out of a collaboration with a junior student. I proposed the initial idea of using symbolic scene descriptions to connect images with text-only language models, while he led the implementation and experiments. I serve as the corresponding author.
