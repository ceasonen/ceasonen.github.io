Today I worked through a refresh of my academic homepage with Codex. I wanted visitors to find my research quickly, with a little of my personality still in view. We put my profile and links back in the left sidebar, shortened the education line to my college name, and simplified the footer.

The typography took a few tries. I settled on Outfit for most of the site, Caveat for my name and section headings, and Comic Neue for the introduction. News went back into a rounded, scrollable panel with small icons for scholarships, competitions, and paper acceptances. Usagi came back to the Writing section.

We made the same changes in [Cube Page](https://ceasonen.github.io/cube_page/), the reusable version of my homepage. The commits reached GitHub before the new pages went live. GitHub Pages returned a server error, so we retried the deployments and checked both public sites afterward.
