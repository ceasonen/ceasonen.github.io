Cheng Chen 陈成
Undergraduate · Electrical Engineering本科生 · 电气工程
Tongji University同济大学

I'm an undergrad at Tongji University, majoring in electrical engineering. My research is mostly about multimodal AI and trustworthy AI — building models that work across vision, language, and audio, and making them reliable enough to actually use.

I'm looking for PhD opportunities in these areas.

我就读同济大学电气工程专业。本科研究主要做多模态 AI和可信 AI：让模型能处理视觉、语言、音频等多种信息，也让它足够可靠、值得信任。

目前在找这两个方向的博士机会。

2351445(at)tongji.edu.cn Google Scholar GitHub LinkedIn
Research 研究

Multimodal AI — vision-language reasoning, audio-visual understanding, medical imaging. 多模态 AI — 视觉语言推理、音视频理解、医学影像。

Trustworthy AI — model compression, calibration, and knowing when a model might be wrong. 可信 AI — 模型压缩、校准，以及判断模型什么时候可能出错。

PhD Search 博士申请

Looking for a PhD in multimodal learning or trustworthy AI. Happy to talk if your group works on either. 想找多模态学习或可信 AI 方向的博士。如果课题组做这两个方向，欢迎聊聊。

Outside lab: football (Barça & Arsenal), cycling, music. 实验室外：足球（巴萨 & 阿森纳）、骑车、听歌。
