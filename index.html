About

I am an undergraduate student at Tongji University, majoring in Communication Engineering. My research interests include artificial intelligence, multimedia understanding, symbolic reasoning, and wireless intelligence.

I focus on work that is deployable, auditable, and structurally clean, especially at the intersection of multimodal reasoning and communication-aware learning.

Current work includes anonymous projects under review in multimedia understanding and cross-modal reasoning, together with ongoing research on structured LLM reasoning and privacy-aware ISAC.
