Homepage

Deployable trustworthy AI with multimodal and wireless perspectives.

I am an undergraduate researcher at Tongji University in Communication Engineering. My recent work spans clinical knowledge distillation, symbolic visual reasoning, controlled budgeted RAG, neural-operator risk triage, and privacy-aware wireless learning.

I care about research that still holds after the benchmark table: clear problem formulation, careful evaluation, calibration, robustness, and deployment constraints that are treated as first-class design requirements rather than afterthoughts.

I am currently seeking PhD opportunities in multimodal learning, trustworthy AI, medical AI, and deployable learning systems. Groups that value rigorous experiments and strong claim-evidence alignment are the best fit.

Current cycle. IJCAI 2026 accepted co-first work, corresponding-author papers at IJCNN 2026 and ICANN 2026, two ACM MM 2026 submissions with strong initial scores, and first-author work under review at NeurIPS 2026 and IEEE Wireless Communications Letters.
