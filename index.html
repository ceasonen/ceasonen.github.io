Undergraduate · Electrical Engineering
Tongji University

I'm an undergrad at Tongji University, majoring in electrical engineering. My research spans multimodal AI, trustworthy AI, and interdisciplinary AI — building models across vision, language, and audio, making them reliable enough to deploy, and connecting them to domains from clinical screening to scientific computing.

I'm looking for PhD opportunities in these areas, including interdisciplinary programs at the AI–engineering–science interface. 🔭

🔬Research

Multimodal AI — vision-language reasoning, audio-visual understanding, medical imaging.

Trustworthy AI — model compression, calibration, and knowing when a model might be wrong.

Interdisciplinary AI — connecting ML to clinical screening, audio-visual perception, and neural operators for scientific computing.

🔭PhD Search
