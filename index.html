About

My research focuses on multimodal reasoning, efficient deep learning, computer vision, and wireless intelligence / ISAC.

I care about research that stays structurally clean, deployment-aware, and practically usable. Recent public-facing work spans lightweight clinical knowledge transfer, symbolic interfaces for text-only LLM reasoning over visual scenes, and privacy-aware beamforming for sensing-aware wireless systems.

Two multimodal projects are currently under anonymous review at ACM MM, so I describe them only at a high level on this website to avoid exposing details that could interfere with double-blind review.
