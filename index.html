About

I am an undergraduate researcher at Tongji University, where I study Communication Engineering. My work focuses on multimodal intelligence, trustworthy reasoning, and wireless intelligence under realistic deployment constraints.

I am particularly interested in multimedia understanding, structured reasoning with language models, and learning-enabled wireless systems. Across these directions, I care about methods that are accurate, auditable, and practically usable.

Current projects include an under-review co-first-authored study in audio-visual understanding and a first-author under-review study in algorithmic reasoning, together with ongoing work on privacy-aware ISAC and deployment-oriented machine learning.
