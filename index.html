About

My work sits between AI systems and communication intelligence, with an emphasis on models that remain efficient, interpretable, and deployment-ready.

Recent public-facing directions include lightweight knowledge transfer for medical vision, symbolic interfaces for visual reasoning with text-only large language models, and privacy-aware beamforming for integrated sensing and communication.

Anonymous submissions are intentionally summarized only at a high level so that the site stays compatible with double-blind review constraints.
