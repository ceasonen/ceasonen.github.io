Cheng Chen 陈成
Undergraduate Researcher本科研究者
Tongji University同济大学

I am an undergraduate researcher at Tongji University in Communication Engineering. My work sits at the intersection of deployable trustworthy AI and multimodal intelligence, with recent projects in medical model compression, open-vocabulary audio-visual understanding, budgeted RAG, neural-operator risk assessment, and wireless-intelligence privacy.

I am currently looking for PhD opportunities. I hope to work with advisors who care about method design, careful evaluation, calibration, robustness, and whether a system can survive real deployment constraints.

我目前在同济大学通信工程专业学习，研究主线是可部署可信 AI 与多模态智能。近期工作覆盖医学模型压缩、开放词汇音视频理解、预算受限 RAG、神经算子风险评估，以及无线智能隐私保护。

我正在寻找博士阶段的研究机会，希望加入真正重视方法设计、严格评测、校准、鲁棒性和真实部署约束的课题组。

Research Map 研究地图
  • Core:核心： Deployable trustworthy AI (model compression, calibration, risk triage, and robustness under practical budgets). 可部署可信 AI：模型压缩、校准、风险分诊，以及实际预算下的鲁棒性。
  • Modal:模态： Multimodal intelligence (medical imaging, vision-language reasoning, audio-visual event understanding, budgeted RAG, and learning-assisted wireless privacy). 多模态智能：医学影像、视觉语言推理、音视频事件理解、预算受限 RAG 与无线智能隐私保护。
  • In Review:在审/进行中： ACM MM 2026 submissions are described by research direction in the current review cycle; the NeurIPS 2026 work is kept as ongoing research. ACM MM 2026 在审投稿以研究方向与初评分展示；NeurIPS 2026 工作仅作为 ongoing research 展示。
PhD Search 招博意向
  • Seeking PhD opportunities in multimodal learning, trustworthy AI, medical AI, and deployable learning systems. 寻找多模态学习、可信 AI、医学 AI 与可部署学习系统方向的博士机会。
  • Best Fit:期望： Groups that value rigorous experiments, clean problem formulation, and systems that can be evaluated beyond a single benchmark table. 希望加入重视严格实验、清晰问题定义，并愿意把系统放到真实约束下检验的课题组。
