About

I am an undergraduate researcher at Tongji University, where I study Communication Engineering. My research centers on deployable trustworthy AI and multimodal intelligence, with a focus on reliable understanding, compression, and deployment in medical, audio-visual, and scientific-computing settings.

My current work spans lightweight knowledge distillation for medical imaging, multimodal and vision-language reasoning, open-vocabulary audio-visual event localization, and neural-operator risk assessment.

Going forward, I hope to study medical multimodal foundation models, vision-language reasoning, audio-video multimodal understanding, model compression, and trustworthy deployment.

Yi Xu*†, Cheng Chen*, Mufan Cao

International Joint Conference on Deployable Trustworthy AI (IJCAI)

Cheng Chen, Yi Xu, Wenzhuo Lei

NeurIPS (Under Review)

A first-author framework for single-pass risk assessment in neural operators using prediction-path allocation entropy, designed for trustworthy deployment without extra uncertainty heads.
