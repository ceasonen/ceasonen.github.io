About

I am an undergraduate researcher at Tongji University in Communication Engineering. My work sits at the intersection of deployable trustworthy AI and multimodal intelligence, with recent projects in medical model compression, open-vocabulary audio-visual understanding, robust OCR/RAG, neural-operator risk assessment, and wireless-intelligence privacy.

I am currently looking for PhD opportunities. I hope to work with advisors who care about method design, careful evaluation, calibration, robustness, and whether a system can survive real deployment constraints.
