IJCAI 2026

OrthKD: Extracting Generalized Clinical Knowledge from Heterogeneous Teachers for Lightweight Deployment

Yi Xu, Cheng Chen, Mufan Cao

International Joint Conference on Artificial Intelligence

OrthKD studies lightweight deployment under heterogeneous-teacher distillation, using complementary teacher signals to improve robust diabetic retinopathy screening on compact student models.

Submitted to IEEE WCL

Learning-Aided Privacy Beamforming for ISAC

Cheng Chen, Fan Xu

IEEE Wireless Communications Letters

This work formulates privacy-aware beamforming for ISAC with uncertainty-aware leakage suppression, an exact offline solver, and a lightweight online deployment strategy.

Submitted to IJCNN 2026

Enabling Visual Reasoning in Text-Only LLMs via Symbolic Scene Descriptions

Wenzhuo Lei, Mufan Cao, Chaorong Ye, Yi Xu, Cheng Chen*

International Joint Conference on Neural Networks

DeepYOLO converts images into compact symbolic scene reports so that a text-only LLM can perform visual reasoning without multimodal pre-training.
