Stay updated with my latest research, submissions, awards, and academic milestones.

2026

2026.05
Two ACM MM 2026 submissions received initial scores of 4/4/4/4/3 (approx. top 5%; co-first author) and 4/4/3/2 (approx. top 15--20%; first author), covering open-vocabulary audio-visual localization and transferable visual security.两篇 ACM MM 2026 在审投稿初评分为 4/4/4/4/3（约 Top 5%，共同第一作者）与 4/4/3/2（约 15--20%，第一作者），方向覆盖开放词汇视听事件定位与视觉安全可迁移攻击。
2026.05
The counter-evidence planning work for controlled budgeted RAG was accepted to ICANN 2026; Cheng Chen is corresponding author.预算受限 RAG 反证据规划工作被 ICANN 2026 录用，陈成为通讯作者，研究固定预算下的可靠检索决策。
2026.04
OrthKD was accepted to IJCAI 2026 as an oral presentation. The work studies heterogeneous-teacher knowledge distillation for lightweight medical AI deployment.OrthKD 被 IJCAI 2026 录用为 Oral Presentation，研究面向轻量化医学 AI 部署的异构教师知识蒸馏。
2026.04
A first-author NeurIPS 2026 submission studies single-pass risk triage and deployable risk assessment for neural operators.NeurIPS 2026 第一作者在投工作研究单次前向风险分诊与可部署风险评估，面向神经算子的早期预警与低开销部署。
2026.03
A symbolic scene-description pipeline for enabling visual reasoning in text-only LLMs was accepted to IJCNN 2026; Cheng Chen is corresponding author.面向纯文本 LLM 的符号化场景描述视觉推理工作被 IJCNN 2026 录用，陈成为通讯作者。
2026.01
A first-author IEEE Wireless Communications Letters submission studies privacy-aware beamforming under eavesdropper location uncertainty.IEEE Wireless Communications Letters 第一作者在投工作研究窃听者位置不确定条件下的隐私感知波束赋形。
